Dr. Johnathon Abramson

Jonathan Abramson brings together technology consulting, academic leadership, and more than 15 years of experience in higher education. His background spans enterprise databases, reporting systems, and IT leadership in banking, commercial consulting, and education. He has led academic programs, developed online curricula, and supported faculty in using instructional technology effectively. At AEC Strategies, he applies that experience to online course development, AI-enabled learning, curriculum digitization, and LMS integration. Jonathan holds a Doctor of Computer Science in Enterprise Information Systems from Colorado Technical University and a master’s degree in Information Systems.

Kevin Brown Moreno, MAT

Kevin Brown Moreno brings together expertise in education, instructional design, artificial intelligence, and technology consulting. A returned United States Peace Corps volunteer, Kevin brings an international perspective to educational innovation and technology integration. His background spans curriculum development, digital learning, software development, machine learning, and AI-driven automation. At AEC Strategies, he applies this interdisciplinary experience to developing innovative educational solutions, integrating emerging technologies, and transforming traditional instructional content into engaging digital learning experiences. Kevin holds a Master of Arts in Teaching (MAT) in Curriculum and Instruction from Brescia University and is completing a Master of Science in Information Systems with an emphasis in Artificial Intelligence.
